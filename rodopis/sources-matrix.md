# Sources matrix

What to try for each person, by place, period and record type. The goal is not to confirm a tree card but to find everything that exists about the person and the people around them. An empty result is written down ("searched, nothing": source, query, years) so nobody repeats it.

Coverage is strongest for Bohemia, Moravia, Slovakia, Upper Hungary and Austria. For other countries, use the structure (sections 2 and 3) and swap in local sources.

## 1. Sources by type

Without a browser (WebSearch / WebFetch) more works than one would expect: full-text newspapers and indexed databases can be searched from name + year + region, even without knowing the exact place.

## 1b. Finders, indexes and regional tips

**Finding the right register and archive (first step for a new place)**
- matriky.online: register of about 100,000 parish books from 8 Czech archives, search by place or parish, links straight to the archive viewers.
- map.heritagefilms.cz/farnosti/: Catholic, Protestant and Jewish parish for each place, coloured by archive; heritagefilms.cz/mapa: archive districts.
- pfarren-cz.oefr.at/karte/: parishes before 1918.
- Catholic and Protestant registers of the same place are often in different archives.

**Indexes and databases (Czech lands and Austria)**
- genteam.at (free after registration): indexes for Vienna, Lower and Upper Austria, Moravia; obituaries from Prager Tagblatt; gazetteer with parish.
- zanikleobce.cz: vanished villages, Sudetendeutsches Adressbuch 1939 scans, map with historical layers.
- myczechroots.com recruitment locality index: replacement regiment by place, use it BEFORE searching the Military Archives.
- Hungaricana Militär-Schematismus 1791 to 1914: officers and officials only.
- Casualty lists of 1866 in Národní listy (OCR in digitalniknihovna.cz).
- chewra.com (Keshet project): Jewish cemeteries in Bohemia and Moravia.
- genealogyindexer.org/directories: address books, strong on Jewish names.
- archiv.ucl.cas.cz: newspaper and journal archive of the Czech Academy of Sciences.
- nasejmena.cz: frequency and distribution of Czech surnames.
- ANNO and Kramerius return different hits for the same query; try both.

**Slovakia and Upper Hungary**
- Twin place names are common (Holíč in Slovakia vs Holice near Dunajská Streda). Always note the Hungarian and German name of the place.
- jewishgen.org/databases/Hungary/: census 1869, Jewish census 1848, tax list 1828, Slovak civil vital records, 1930 census of Jews in Slovakia, JOWBR. Free account.
- KehilaLink pages per community (registers, settlers, cemeteries) where they exist.
- Pinkas Slovakia (JewishGen Yizkor translation).
- slovak-jewish-heritage.org; ESJF for Jewish cemeteries.
- CJH LibGuide "Slovak primary records": Slovak National Archives, Auslander index (1993), Sarmanyová "Cirkevné matriky na Slovensku"; soldiers 1868 to 1918 = HL Budapest.
- scitacieharky.sk (census 1940 and 1930, community index; Jewish households indexed separately, non-Jewish members missing), slovakiana.sk (census 1930).
- csaladfa.palmai.eu/felvidekiak: which village belonged to which parish; slovnik.juls.savba.sk: historical place names (search Hungaricana urbaria under every variant).
- adatbazisokonline.hu: 1715 census (try endings -falva / -falu; transcriptions are faulty, better browse by county).
- geoportal.gov.sk: military surveys I to III free (Arcanum maps and newspapers are paid).
- rahprojekt.elte.hu: students from Hungary 1100 to 1850; cemetery.sk: graves in Slovakia.
- Hungarian gazetteer 1913, free in the FamilySearch Digital Library.

**Hungary:** eleveltar.hu works only from Hungarian IP addresses.

## 1c. Reading Kurrent, Latin and terms

- genealogie.nka.cz (Rukověť genealoga): Kurrent, Fraktur, abbreviations, units, currencies, German and Latin dictionaries, month names, causes of death.
- genea.cz/slovnicky, genea.cz/linkovna; genebaze.cz term list.
- transkribus.org (model for 19th century German Kurrent: usable, not perfect); kurent.bastan.cz and deutsche-handschrift.de (render a word in Kurrent to compare letter shapes); woerterbuchnetz.de (old German words); mateo.uni-mannheim.de/camenaref/siglalatina.html (Latin abbreviations).

**Where to ask for help with reading** (only the researcher posts; agents prepare the question and the crop, never register or post anywhere)
- genealogie.taby.cz forum "Čtení starých materiálů"; forum.geni.sk "Čítanie starých dokumentov" and "geografické názvy".
- community.familysearch.org, group "Czechia Research" (translation requests).

## 2. Mining a record for leads

Every record read is mined for **leads**, not only for the fact asked for:

- **FAN principle (Friends, Associates, Neighbours):** godparents, witnesses, officiant, midwife, neighbours in the same house number. A godparent with the same surname is almost certainly a relative; a godparent who returns across all children is close family or a close tie.
- **House number** is the family's identity over generations: who lived there before and after, who bought or inherited it (cadastre, land book, census).
- **Occupation** leads to further sources: guild books, trade registers, firm histories, local newspapers.
- **Marginal notes** (leaving the church, marriage elsewhere, legitimation, death) point to another record somewhere else. Each note is a lead.
- **A mismatch** (age, place, name) is not an error to throw away but a hypothesis: a same-name sibling who died young? a second marriage? a move?
- **Events in the place** in key years (fire, cholera, war, flood, railway, factory) explain moves and deaths.

## 3. What a research member returns

1. **The story** of the person or family in prose, each sentence with its source and confidence grade (`rodopis/rules.md`, section "Confidence scale").
2. **Facts for the tree** (event, date, place, house number, source, URL or reference).
3. **Searched, nothing** (source + query + years), so it is not repeated.
4. **Leads and connections:** new people, house numbers, kinship hypotheses, each with the next concrete step (what exactly to open).
5. **To open in the browser** for the lead (archive, volume, reference, years, image, URL) and **images to download** (URL + licence).

Never guess a link; leave it out rather than invent it. Members write nothing into the tree and use no browser.
