# Archive letter skeleton

rod-archivy fills this in per addressee, one letter for all gaps aimed at the same institution. Values in angle brackets come from the brief, the person's `status.md` and the archive register; nothing is guessed. Saved as `genealogy/archives/letters/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<institution-slug>.md` (or a Gmail draft when `gmail_drafts: on`). The researcher reviews and sends.

## File layout

```
To: <verified address> (source: <register row / official website URL>)
Subject: <request type>: <surname>, <place>, <years>

<letter body>

---
Request log rows: <#, #>
Checked against the register: does not hold <...>, how to apply <...>
```

## Body, English

```
Dear Sir or Madam,

I am researching my own family history and would like to ask for your help with the following records.

Person: <full name> (also recorded as <variants>), born <date> in <place>, house no. <n>, son/daughter of <parents>.
What we already know: <fact> (<source, reference, image>).
What I am asking for: <record type> in <fonds / register>, years <from to>, place <place>.

If you hold these records, could you please let me know the fee and how to pay? A scan or copy of the whole entry would be ideal.
If the records are held elsewhere, I would be grateful for a pointer.

Thank you very much for your work and your time.

Kind regards,
<researcher name and contact as given by the researcher>
```

## Body, Czech

```
Dobrý den,

zpracovávám vlastní rodokmen a chtěl(a) bych Vás poprosit o pomoc s následujícími záznamy.

Osoba: <celé jméno> (zapisováno také jako <varianty>), narozen(a) <datum> v <místo>, čp. <n>, syn/dcera <rodiče>.
Co už víme: <fakt> (<pramen, signatura, snímek>).
Oč žádám: <typ záznamu> ve fondu <fond / matrika>, roky <od do>, obec <obec>.

Pokud záznamy máte, mohl(a) bych poprosit o informaci o poplatku a způsobu platby? Ideální by byl sken nebo kopie celého zápisu.
Pokud jsou záznamy uloženy jinde, budu vděčný/vděčná za nasměrování.

Děkuji za Vaši práci a čas.

S pozdravem
<jméno a kontakt badatele, jak je zadal>
```

## Checks before the letter is final

- The institution takes e-mail or letters (not a form only).
- The register does not list these records as "does not hold".
- No request for the same thing is already running.
- Every name variant is listed.
- Every known fact has its source; no guess is written as a fact.
- The address is verified.
