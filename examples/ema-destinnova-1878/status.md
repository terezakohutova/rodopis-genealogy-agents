## Key facts (3-8 lines, copied into member briefs)

All of these come from encyclopedias and biographies, none yet from an original record.

- Born 26 February 1878 in Prague as Emilie Pavlína Kittlová; father Emanuel Kittl
- Opera singer (soprano); stage name Destinn after her teacher Marie Loewe-Destinn
- Bought the chateau at Stráž nad Nežárkou in 1914; confined there by the Austrian authorities during World War I
- Married 1923 Josef Halfter, an air force officer
- Died 28 January 1930 in České Budějovice; buried at Vyšehrad cemetery, Prague
